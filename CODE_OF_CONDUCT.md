# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors and learners, regardless of background, experience level, identity, or perspective.

---

## Standards

Contributors are expected to uphold the following standards:

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other members of the community.
- Respect differing viewpoints, approaches, and constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the overall learning community.
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism and suggestions.
- Write clean, well-documented code that benefits others.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are considered unacceptable:

- Harassment, trolling, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Disrespectful or exclusionary conduct in issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Publishing private information of others without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or educational setting.

---
